# How this meme was made

The joke is the classic two-buttons dilemma, aimed at the thirteen-year
wait since GTA V: one button says "LET ROCKSTAR COOK", the other says
"I'M ALREADY COOKED", and the fan — grey, baggy-eyed, cloth pressed to
his forehead — can't bring himself to press either one. His finger hovers
in the gap between the buttons, touching neither.

The picture is 1200x1200, drawn entirely in code with Python PIL — no
screenshots, no stock art, no image model. Every element is a shape the
script draws: the charcoal console and its two red buttons, the fan's
oversized head (receding hair, raised inner eyebrows, eyes rolled up
toward the buttons, clenched zigzag mouth, stubble, purple eye bags),
the three cyan sweat drops, and the make.fun ghost in the lower-right
corner, tilted toward the fan with a sweat drop of its own and the
"make.fun" wordmark beneath it. Flat colors, thick black outlines, warm
off-white background.

Type is Noto Sans Display Condensed Black: the header, the button labels
and the wordmark are all set in it, sized so every word still reads when
the picture is shrunk to 300x300. I checked the render at full size and
at 300x300 before calling it done — both choices, the expression and the
ghost all register at a glance.
